PROCESS

Five steps, and an audit log throughout.

The portal implements the evaluation lifecycle end to end. Every state change is recorded with the actor, the time and the address it came from.

1 · Register

An organisation creates an account. The first user becomes the organisation's administrator, and may sign the agreement and request evaluations. Passwords are hashed with Argon2id; the plaintext is never stored.

2 · Sign the agreement

The confidential evaluation agreement is presented in full and accepted explicitly. The version and the SHA-256 digest of the accepted text are recorded, so a later change to the document cannot retroactively alter what was agreed to. The agreement covers what the package may be used for, that the results may be published by the evaluator, that the package itself may not be disclosed, and that it must be returned or destroyed at the end of the evaluation.

3 · Request an evaluation

The organisation names the codec it wants to test against, the content it plans to use, its sampling plan, and what it expects the mechanism to do. A reviewer approves or declines the request, and the decision and its note are recorded.

4 · Download the package

An approved organisation may request a download for any gated artifact. The link is signed, time-limited, bound to the artifact's digest and bound to the requesting user, so it cannot be edited to fetch a different artifact, cannot be reused by another user, and stops working when it expires. The artifact's checksum is verified against the recorded digest.

5 · Submit results

The organisation uploads its results and its own account of what it measured, including any BD-rate it computed. A reviewer accepts or rejects the submission, and an accepted submission closes the evaluation.

What the audit log is for. It is append-only, and the application never updates or deletes a row in it. It is the record of who was given what and when, which is what a confidentiality dispute turns on.
