Why an evaluation rather than a paper

The published numbers come from GIRC, a research codec with a small mode set and integer-sample motion, and they put the mechanism at a 0.175% saving on the luma plane and 0.104% on the whole frame, on nine sequences of twelve. That result is a starting point for an argument, not the end of one. A codec team with a real VVC or AV1 encoder can test the mechanism where the transform set is larger, where the residual coding is richer, and where inter prediction is in play.

The evaluation gives you the reference implementation, the trained predictor and the benchmark harness, so the comparison runs on your content and against your encoder rather than on ours.

What you receive

Reference codec

The full Rust implementation with both signalling modes behind one interface, its test suite, and the oracle that checks the transform arithmetic against VVenC bit for bit.

Trained predictor

The model, the training set, its checksum and the pipeline that produced it. The method, not only the weights, is what is being evaluated.

Benchmark harness

The tool behind the published figures, with the anchor codecs and the Bjontegaard implementation, so the evaluation is reproducible on your own material.

Patent materials

The draft specification, the claim set, the drawings and the prior-art analysis, in the form a patent counsel would need to assess the filing.

What we ask of you

Keep it confidential

The package and the materials are the evaluator's confidential information. You may use them to run the evaluation and may publish your results.

Report honestly

Including when the mechanism does not help. A negative result is worth more to the filing than a flattering one, because it establishes the mechanism's real envelope.

Return it afterwards

At the end of the evaluation the package is destroyed and the destruction confirmed. The results stay with us.

Talk to us

Especially about the case where the mechanism fails. That is where the claim set has to be drawn carefully, and your evidence is what draws it.

A research evaluation, not a licence. Nothing here grants any right in the evaluator's intellectual property. A licence or an assignment is a separate conversation, and it follows the evaluation rather than preceding it.
