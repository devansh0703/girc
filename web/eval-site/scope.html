SCOPE

What the evaluation covers, and what it does not.

Stating the boundary precisely is what makes the result usable. A patent attorney and a codec engineer both need to know exactly what was and was not tested.

Covered

  • The geometry-inference transform signalling mechanism, in the explicit and inferred forms, in the reference implementation.
  • The predictor: the training method, the training set, the resulting model, and the reproducibility manifest.
  • The rate-distortion margin: the encoder's trade between signalling the transform and distorting the reconstruction, swept rather than fixed.
  • The benchmark harness, so the evaluation can be reproduced on your own content and against your own encoders.

Not covered

  • A VVC-conformant bitstream. GIRC's format is its own, and the evaluation is about the mechanism rather than about interoperability.
  • A performance claim. GIRC is a research codec and is not a VVC competitor; the anchor comparison is context.
  • Any grant of rights. The evaluation grants no licence and no assignment.
  • Inter prediction, which GIRC does not implement. The mechanism's behaviour with a motion-compensated residual is an open question the evaluation is well placed to answer.

What we would most like to learn

The mechanism's published result is a 0.236% saving over twelve sequences, and the reason it is not larger is measurable: the joint transform mode is barely predictable from the causal neighbourhood, because the rate-distortion choice between the modes is close to a tie. The explicit signalling is already nearly free for a context-adaptive coder to code, so the distortion the inference costs comes close to the rate it saves. That is the number the evaluation is meant to move.

The three things most likely to change that are outside what GIRC can test, and are exactly what an evaluation with a real codec can:

A larger transform set

The overhead the mechanism removes grows with the number of choices to signal and with how evenly they are distributed.

Inter prediction

A motion-compensated residual carries the motion vector's structure, which is more directional and more predictable from the causal neighbourhood than an intra residual.

Signalling at a coarser grain

One transform per region rather than per block makes the prediction easier and the overhead larger.
