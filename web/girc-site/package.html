CONFIDENTIAL EVALUATION PACKAGE

Test the method against your own codec.

The package is the reference implementation, the trained predictor, the benchmark harness and the draft patent materials, under a confidential evaluation agreement.

What the package contains

Reference codec

The full Rust implementation: VVC-exact integer transforms, intra prediction, quantization, the context-adaptive entropy coder, and both signalling modes. The build, the test suite and the oracle that checks the transforms against VVenC are included.

Trained predictor

The model, the training set it was fitted on, the checksum of that set, and the training pipeline that produced it. The model is a protected artefact, and the method that produced it is the thing being evaluated.

Benchmark harness

The tool that produced the published numbers, with the anchor codecs and the Bjontegaard implementation, so the evaluation can be run on your own content rather than only on ours.

Patent materials

The draft specification, the claim set, the drawings and the prior-art analysis, in the form a codec company's patent counsel would need to evaluate the filing.

How the evaluation runs

  1. An organisation registers, and one of its users signs the confidential evaluation agreement. The version and the digest of the accepted text are recorded.
  2. The organisation requests an evaluation, naming the codec it wants to test against and the content it plans to use.
  3. Once the request is approved, the gated artifacts become available. Downloads are signed, time-limited, and bound to the artifact's digest, so a link cannot be edited to fetch something else or replayed after it expires.
  4. The organisation uploads its results and its own account of what it measured.
  5. A reviewer accepts the results and issues the evaluation report.

Every step is written to an append-only audit log, with the actor, the time and the address the action came from.

What the evaluation is for

The question the package exists to answer is whether the mechanism helps in a codec that is not GIRC. GIRC is an intra-only research codec, and its published numbers are close to break-even. A codec team with a real VVC or AV1 encoder can tell whether the mechanism behaves differently when there are more transform types, when the residual coding is richer, and when inter prediction is in play.

The published results are the starting point for that argument, not the conclusion of it. The margin sweep, the predictability ceiling and the per-sequence breakdown are all included, so the evaluation starts from the same evidence rather than from a claim.

Terms

The agreement covers what may be done with the package, that the results may be used freely by the evaluator, that the package itself may not be disclosed, and that the recipient must return or destroy the package at the end of the evaluation.

It is a research evaluation, not a licence. A licence or an assignment is a separate conversation, and it follows the evaluation rather than preceding it.
