The patent materials

Published rather than gated

The disclosure, the claim set and the prior-art analysis are in the repository under research/patent-package/, and the claim text of the ten references was extracted from the publications themselves rather than summarised. Every quotation in the chart is checked against the extracted text by the test suite.

They are published because the value of this project is that its claims can be checked. A claim behind an agreement is a claim nobody has checked.
