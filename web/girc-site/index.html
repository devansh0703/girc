REFERENCE CODEC · INFERRED TRANSFORM SIGNALLING

The decoder already knows which transform you meant.

Every video codec signals the transform type for every block, even though the choice is almost always the same one. GIRC replaces that signalling with an inference: the decoder predicts the transform from the geometry it has already reconstructed, and the encoder spends a single bit only when the prediction was wrong.

Read the method See the measurements Request the evaluation package

The problem

A VVC block is coded with one of three transforms: DCT-II, DCT-VIII or DST-VII. The encoder picks one by rate-distortion search, and the decoder has no way of knowing which one it picked except that the bitstream tells it. The signalling is a few bits per block, but it is pure overhead: it carries no information the decoder could not have derived for itself.

The reason it cannot be derived is that the choice looks random. It depends on the block's own residual, which the decoder has not yet seen. The encoder looks at the residual, the decoder cannot.

The idea

The decoder can see something the residual does not determine: the shape of the content it has already reconstructed. A block that sits in a directional neighbourhood tends to want a directional transform, and a block in a flat one tends to want the default. That signal is available on both sides at the same moment, so it can carry the choice.

1 · Infer

The decoder reads a two-dimensional causal window of its own reconstruction and predicts the transform from its geometry: the gradient orientation field, the per-axis energies, the intra prediction mode and the quantization parameter.

2 · Signal

The encoder spends one bin on whether the prediction was right. When it was wrong, one further bin selects between the two transforms the prediction ruled out.

3 · Trade

The encoder may take a slightly worse transform than its search found, when doing so costs less than the override would. That margin is the mechanism's rate-distortion knob, and the benchmark sweeps it.

What is in the repository

Reference codec

A Rust implementation of the full path: VVC-exact integer transforms, intra prediction, quantization, a context-adaptive entropy coder, and both signalling modes behind one command-line interface.

Training pipeline

The predictor is trained on the encoder's own decisions, not on a proxy. The pipeline reports the predictability ceiling alongside the shipped model's accuracy.

Benchmark harness

GIRC in both modes against x265, SVT-AV1 and VVenC on the same content, with the Bjontegaard delta rate computed the way the JVET common test conditions define it.

Evaluation portal

A confidential evaluation environment: organisation accounts, versioned agreement acceptance, gated artifact delivery, result submission and an append-only audit log.

On honesty of measurement. The bitstream is GIRC's own research format, not VVC-conformant, and the report says so. The transforms are bit-exact against Fraunhofer HHI's VVenC, so the transform arithmetic is a real one, but GIRC is an intra-only research codec and is not a VVC competitor. The numbers it reports are about the signalling mechanism, measured under a controlled comparison.
