Scope

What the evaluation covers, and what it does not.

Stating the boundary precisely is what makes the result usable. A patent attorney and a codec engineer both need to know exactly what was and was not tested.

Covered

What is in scope

The mechanism

The geometry-inference transform signalling, in the explicit and inferred forms, in the reference implementation. Both syntaxes, and the rate the explicit mode costs per block against the standard's own.

The predictor

The training method, the training set, the resulting model, the predictability ceiling over the same features, and the reproducibility manifest.

The margin

The encoder's trade between signalling the transform and distorting the reconstruction, swept rather than fixed, and reported as the whole trade.

The benchmark harness

So the evaluation can be reproduced on your own content and against your own encoders, with the anchor codecs and the Bjontegaard implementation included.

Not covered

What is out of scope

Out of scope Why
A VVC-conformant bitstream GIRC's format is its own. The evaluation is about the mechanism rather than about interoperability, and a conformant stream would mean a conformant H.266 encoder, which is a different and much larger piece of work.
A performance claim GIRC is a research codec with a small mode set and integer-sample motion. The anchor comparison is context, and the scale of the gap is published rather than hidden.
Any grant of rights The evaluation grants no licence and no assignment. A licence is a separate conversation that follows the evaluation.
A freedom-to-operate opinion A claim-level public-record search is not an FTO opinion, and the national phase filings of the families in the set were not searched. That needs a patent attorney.

The open question

What we would most like to learn

The mechanism's published result is a 0.236% saving over twelve sequences, and the reason it is not larger is measurable: the joint transform mode is barely predictable from the causal neighbourhood, because the rate-distortion choice between the modes is close to a tie. The explicit signalling is already nearly free for a context-adaptive coder to code, so the distortion the inference costs comes close to the rate it saves.

That is the number the evaluation is meant to move, and the three things most likely to move it are outside what GIRC can test.

A larger transform set

The overhead the mechanism removes grows with the number of choices to signal and with how evenly they are distributed, so a codec with more transform types has more to save.

Inter prediction

A motion-compensated residual carries the motion vector's structure, which is more directional and more predictable from the causal neighbourhood than an intra residual.

A richer residual coder

The modes separate more when the residual coder is richer, so the rate-distortion choice stops being a tie and the predictor has something to find.

Signalling at a coarser grain

One transform per region rather than per block makes the prediction easier and the overhead larger.
