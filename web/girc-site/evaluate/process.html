Process

Five steps, and an audit log throughout.

The portal implements the evaluation lifecycle end to end. Every state change is recorded with the actor, the time and the address it came from.

The lifecycle

Register, agree, request, download, submit

{{FIGURE:fig-evaluation-lifecycle}}
01

Register

An organisation creates an account. The first user becomes the organisation's administrator. Passwords are hashed with Argon2id; the plaintext is never stored.

02

Sign the agreement

The agreement is presented in full and accepted explicitly. The version and the SHA-256 digest of the accepted text are recorded, so a later change to the document cannot retroactively alter what was agreed to.

03

Request an evaluation

The organisation names the codec it wants to test against, the content it plans to use, its sampling plan, and what it expects the mechanism to do.

04

Download the package

Once approved, the gated artifacts become available. The link is signed, time-limited, bound to the artifact's digest and bound to the requesting user.

05

Submit results

The organisation uploads its results and its own account of what it measured, including any BD-rate it computed. A reviewer accepts or rejects the submission.

The agreement

What it covers, and what it does not

The agreement covers what the package may be used for, that the results may be published freely by the evaluator, that the package itself may not be disclosed, and that the recipient must return or destroy the package at the end of the evaluation.

It is a research evaluation, not a licence. A licence or an assignment is a separate conversation, and it follows the evaluation rather than preceding it.

The audit log is append-only

The application never updates or deletes a row in it. It is the record of who was given what and when, which is what a confidentiality dispute turns on.

The hosted analysis

The shorter path, when you do not need the package

If the question is whether the measurement says anything about your content, the hosted analysis answers it without the lifecycle at all: post a sequence, get the figures, and nothing is retained. It is the same code the portal runs.
