About

One maintainer, one repository, and a service that costs nothing to use.

GIRC is built by Devansh Raulo. It is not a company, there is no commercial licence to buy, and there is nothing on this site gated behind a price or a request form. The codec, the training pipeline, the benchmark and the portal are all here, and the fastest way to get an answer about any of them is to email me directly.

The reason it is built this way is simple: a measurement is only worth as much as somebody else's ability to check it. A library you cannot read the source of, and a number you cannot re-run, is a claim. Everything about how this project is published follows from trying not to make claims.

What the project is for

A wrong optimisation is expensive; knowing which one not to make is cheap

The whole reason this exists is the figure below. A codec team spends its time on the entropy coder, the motion search and the residual coding, and the transform type is one line of syntax that costs a few bins per block. Nobody optimises it because nobody can see what it costs.

GIRC makes that cost visible and then removes the part of it that is removable. The measurement is small because the overhead is small, and saying so plainly is more useful than a larger number that does not survive being checked.

How it is published

Open source, free to use, and verifiable

The licence

Apache-2.0, the whole of the code: the codec, the training pipeline, the benchmark, the portal and the site. There is no separate commercial licence and no source-available tier, because a project whose value is that its claims can be checked cannot afford one part of itself that cannot be checked.

The patent materials

The disclosure, the claim set and the prior-art analysis are published in the repository rather than released under an evaluation agreement. A claim is only worth as much as somebody else's ability to check it, and the claim text of the ten references is in the repository too.

The hosted service

The analysis runs on one free instance, kept awake by an uptime monitor, because a free workspace gets 750 hours a month and a month is 730 to 744 hours. The code it runs is the code in the repository, and every limit it has is published on the service page rather than discovered by being rejected.

The release discipline

One commit per change, with a subject that says what changed and a body that says why. The numbers in the release notes are the numbers the report holds, and the script that checks them runs in CI.

Where it is not finished

Stated here rather than hidden

Open item Why it is open
A VVC-conformant bitstream GIRC measures the mechanism with its own format, which isolates the signalling and nothing else. What is conformant is the signalling the mechanism removes, checked against the reference encoder over 187,834 transform units.
A counsel-led freedom-to-operate search A public-record claim-level search is not an FTO opinion, and the national phase filings of the families in the set were not searched. That needs a patent attorney.
A richer residual coder The modes separate more when the residual coder is richer, which is where the mechanism should pay. It is the direction the work points and it is not done.
A larger causal window The features read a 16-sample window. A larger one, or a learned representation of the neighbourhood, would raise the predictability ceiling.
