1. A method, comprising:
obtaining, for at least one video block, syntax information indicative of use of an implicit multiple transform
selection mode;
obtaining a first indication of a low frequency non-separable transform mode and/or a second indication of a
matrix-based intra prediction mode;
encoding the at least one video block in a bitstream using at least one transform of the multiple transform
selection mode, wherein the at least one transform is selected based on the first indication and/or the second
indication; and
storing, conveying, or transmitting the bitstream comprising the encoded at least one video block.
2. An apparatus, comprising:
a processor, configured to perform:
obtaining, for at least one video block, syntax information indicative of use of an implicit multiple transform
selection mode;
obtaining a first indication of a low frequency non-separable transform mode and/or a second indication of a
matrix-based intra prediction mode;
encoding the at least one video block in a bitstream using at least one transform of the multiple transform
selection mode, wherein the at least one transform is selected based on the first indication and/or the second
indication; and
storing, conveying, or transmitting the bitstream comprising the encoded at least one video block.
3. A method, comprising:
determining, for at least one video block in a bitstream, syntax information indicative of use of an implicit multiple
transform selection mode;
determining a first indication of a low frequency non-separable transform mode and/or a second indication of
a matrix-based intra prediction mode; and
decoding the at least one video block in said bitstream using at least one transform of themultiple transform
